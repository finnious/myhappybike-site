Journey of Hope Cyclist Finnious Scott Finney

Summer of 2017, Scott Finney was a cyclist on the Trans-America Journey of Hope Team. The 38 member team rode bicycles 4,330 miles coast to coast from Seattle Washington to Washington D.C. They rode to raise money and awareness for people with disabilities. Most of the team members had not met before arriving in Seattle the beginning of June. All members of Pi Kappa Phi Fraternity, they were from all over the nation and had joined chapters all over the nation.

I don’t recall when or when I bought this domain name, but it fits a website that talks about bicycle stuff.
